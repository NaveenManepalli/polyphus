Solo founders
You have the idea. Not the time.

You already know what you want to build. What you don't have is six months, a co-founder, and the money for either.

  • Start from one sentence, not a spec document
  • The crew keeps working while you are at your day job
  • You approve the big calls and nothing else lands on you
Businesses
You have a backlog a mile long.

The work is scoped and agreed. You just cannot hire fast enough to clear it, and every quarter the list gets longer.

  • Hand a whole project over, not a single ticket
  • Add crews when the work grows, drop them when it doesn't
  • Your team reviews the output instead of writing it
